# Preserve effective-dated habit goals

Daily Targets and Schedules change from tomorrow through Goal Revisions, and each historical date retains the revision that applied to it rather than being recalculated against the latest settings. Repeated edits before the same effective date replace the pending revision, while eligible Activity Entry corrections still recalculate actual results. This prevents a lower target or changed weekday selection from rewriting past completion and Streaks, at the cost of retaining goal history and resolving rules by date.
